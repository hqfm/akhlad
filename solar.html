✨ Wonders of our Solar System

The most extreme and fascinating locations in our neighborhood.

Highest Mountain

Olympus Mons

Located on Mars. It is the tallest volcano in the solar system, standing about 2.5 times the height of Mount Everest. Its uniqueness lies in its massive width and lack of tectonic plate movement on Mars, allowing it to grow uninterrupted.
Deepest/Largest Crater

South Pole-Aitken Basin

Located on Earth's Moon. It is one of the largest and oldest impact craters in the solar system, spanning about 2,500 km wide and 6 to 8 km deep.
Most Asteroid Impacts

Mercury's Surface

Because Mercury lacks a substantial atmosphere to burn up incoming debris, it is the most heavily cratered planet, preserving the violent history of the Late Heavy Bombardment.
Largest Storm

Great Red Spot

A massive anticyclonic storm on Jupiter that has been raging for at least 400 years, large enough to swallow the Earth entirely.

☀ The Sun

The Sun

The Sun contains 99.8% of the mass in the entire solar system. Its gravity holds our solar system together.
